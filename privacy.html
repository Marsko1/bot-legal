# 🔒 Privacy Policy **Last updated: July 31, 2026** This Privacy Policy explains what information the Discord bot (the **"Bot"**) collects, processes, and stores when you invite or use the Bot on a Discord server. By using the Bot, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. ## 1. Information We Collect The Bot may collect, process, or store the following information as necessary to provide its services: * Discord server IDs. * Discord channel IDs. * Discord role IDs. * Server information, such as member counts and other information necessary to determine server eligibility and facilitate partnerships. * Advertisement content submitted through configured advertisement channels. * Partnership and pairing information, including partnership history and cooldown information. * Server configuration settings required for the Bot to function correctly. The Bot only collects and processes information that is reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, and improve its partnership and advertisement services. ## 2. Message Content and Advertisement Channels The Bot may read and process messages sent in **channels that have been specifically configured for advertisements or partnerships**. This access is necessary for the Bot to: * Retrieve advertisement content. * Process advertisements for distribution. * Send advertisements to partnered servers. * Apply automated moderation or filtering. * Manage the Bot's partnership and advertisement functionality. The Bot does not intentionally monitor or process messages from unrelated channels unless required for a specific Bot feature or functionality. Advertisement content may be stored for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's services, manage partnerships, prevent abuse, or maintain operational records. ## 3. How We Use Information The information collected or processed by the Bot may be used to: * Provide and operate the Bot's partnership services. * Pair eligible Discord servers with each other. * Determine server eligibility for partnerships. * Manage partnership cooldowns and prevent repeated pairings. * Retrieve and distribute advertisements between partnered servers. * Store and apply server-specific configuration settings. * Moderate or review advertisement content. * Prevent abuse, spam, or manipulation of the Bot's systems. * Troubleshoot technical issues and maintain the Bot. ## 4. Data Sharing The Bot does not sell or rent collected information to third parties. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the Bot, such as hosting or infrastructure providers. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to: * Comply with applicable laws or legal requirements. * Protect the security and integrity of the Bot. * Investigate abuse, fraud, or malicious activity. * Enforce the Bot's Terms of Service. ## 5. Data Retention Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's services, maintain partnership functionality, prevent abuse, and fulfill legitimate operational or legal requirements. Server configuration information and partnership records may be retained while the Bot continues to provide services to a server. Advertisement content may be retained for the period necessary to operate the advertisement and partnership systems. When information is no longer necessary, it may be deleted or removed from active systems. ## 6. Data Deletion If you want information associated with your Discord server to be deleted, you may contact the Bot's operators through the official support server or designated support channels. Requests may be subject to verification to ensure that the person requesting deletion has appropriate authority over the relevant Discord server. Some information may be retained when necessary for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or legitimate operational purposes. ## 7.
